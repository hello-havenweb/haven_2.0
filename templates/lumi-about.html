STUDIO PRACTICE • ESTABLISHED 2019

PERMANENCE OVER NOVELTY

Operating from parallel studios in Copenhagen and Tokyo, we unite Scandinavian functional restraint with Japanese material sensitivity.

Studio Lumi was founded by Creative Director Astrid Lindqvist and Spatial Architect Kenzo Mori. Having worked for over a decade in global design capitals, we observed that contemporary branding had surrendered to algorithmic sameness: vibrant gradients without conviction, rounded cards without architectural weight, and messaging crafted for fleeting 3-second impressions.

Our response was to construct an intentionally small, highly disciplined studio. We do not maintain account managers, tiered bureaucracy, or speculative pitching teams. When a client commissions Studio Lumi, they collaborate directly with the authors of their work.

We source inspiration from heavy timber joinery, classical Swiss typographic grids, unglazed ceramics, and brutalist concrete volumes. We believe that when an artifact is designed with rigorous proportion and material honesty, it does not require decorative trickery to command attention.
