The Story of Atelier Vintage

Founded in 2018 inside a former 19th-century cabinetmaker's atelier in the heart of the Marais.

Atelier Vintage began as a quiet rebellion against sterile, clinical fine dining. We believe that food tastes deepest when cooked over honest woodfire, served on unglazed stoneware that remembers the artisan's fingers, and paired with living wines that mirror the weather of their vintage.

Our kitchen is commanded by Chef Julien Vance, whose culinary journey spans Basque country hearths, Kyoto kaiseki temples, and Copenhagen cellars. In our open kitchen, there are no electric induction burners or sous-vide water baths. The cooking temperature is governed entirely by the distance between the iron grill and burning oak coals.

Beyond the kitchen, our morning roastery operates with equal reverence. Sourcing fair-relationship green coffee from cooperatives in Sidama and Huila, we roast twice weekly on an antique cast-iron drum to achieve radiant, floral profiles that transition into our evening dessert service.

01. Woodfire Purety

Seasoned French white birch and vintage applewood impart subtle sweetness without overpowering delicate seafood.

02. Direct Terroir

70% of produce harvested within 36 hours of service from certified organic heirloom seed conservancies.

03. Living Cellar

Over 320 natural, low-intervention and biodynamic labels sourced directly from independent family estates.
