Institutional Brief
INSTITUTIONAL HERITAGE

A Quarter-Century of Independent Counsel

Established in 2002 to provide pure, unconflicted corporate strategic advice free from the underwriting conflicts of traditional bulge-bracket universal banks.

Nova Partners was founded on a singular premise: complex capital decisions require advisors whose sole incentive is the long-term value preservation of their client. Unlike universal banks that prioritize balance sheet lending syndications and trading desks, we do not trade against our clients or market proprietary financial products.

Our partners bring an average of 22 years of senior leadership experience across international corporate finance, public regulatory bodies, and premier management consultancies. We combine deep domain expertise in industrial modernization, energy transition, and sovereign wealth with discreet, high-conviction dealmaking.

Headquartered in London with offices in Zurich, New York, and Tokyo, our firm advises a select portfolio of clients across the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

Unconflicted Advice

We have no trading desks, retail brokerage operations, or research distribution arms to skew our counsel.

Senior Partner Immersion

Mandates are personally executed by senior partners from initial diagnostic to final definitive closing documentation.

Fiduciary Discretion

Our client engagements remain strictly confidential until public regulatory filings require statutory disclosure.
