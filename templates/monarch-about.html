CURRICULUM VITAE • GOVERNING ETHOS

A Life at the Nexus of Capital, Statecraft, and Enterprise.

Marcus Sterling has spent over three decades advising leadership at the highest levels of global commerce. Following early leadership in international merchant banking and subsequent service as chief executive of a dual-listed FTSE 100 enterprise, he transitioned to providing confidential, direct counsel to board chairs, enterprise founders, and family office principals.

His perspective was forged during major systemic shocks: the Asian financial dislocation of 1997, the 2008 global banking retrenchment, and the contemporary restructuring of transatlantic industrial supply chains.

Sterling holds advanced degrees in Moral Philosophy from Balliol College, Oxford, and Corporate Law from Cambridge. He is an honorary fellow of the Royal Society of the Arts and serves on the advisory councils of several non-aligned geopolitical research institutes.
